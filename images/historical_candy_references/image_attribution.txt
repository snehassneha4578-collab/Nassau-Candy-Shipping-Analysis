# Chocolate Creams for the American Expeditionary Forces (1918)

Historical photograph description:

"Chocolate Creams for the American Expeditionary Forces. The American Soldier has great fondness and real need for sweets which the Quartermaster Corps meets by manufacturing and selling candy at cost. A soldier who gets plenty of candy seldom craves liquor."

- Location: Paris
- Date: December 26, 1918
- Photographer: U.S. Army Signal Corps
- Source: Library of Congress
- Description reference: Lot-8318-7
- Citation date: 2016/07/22
